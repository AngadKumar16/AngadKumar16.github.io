Background

Cardiovascular disease causes 17.9 million deaths per year worldwide. Current challenges include:

  • 13 Americans die daily awaiting organ transplants
  • 86% of bioprinted constructs fail from vascular necrosis
  • Manual CT annotation takes 8-12 hours per patient
Rising cardiovascular disease deaths over time
Current manual segmentation process performed by trained radiologists

Data Preprocessing

Raw CT scans require windowing and resampling to standardize input:

  • CT window: [-200, 700] HU
  • Target spacing: 0.6 mm³
  • Voxel sparsity: 0.175%

Impact & Future Work

Clinical Impact: Automated pipeline enables scalable, patient-specific cardiac tissue engineering. Radiologists can focus on surgeries rather than manual annotation, addressing the critical organ shortage.
  • Expand to lymphatic networks and multi-organ applications
  • Integrate MRI for increased accessibility (trade-off: lower accuracy)
  • Clinical validation and peer-reviewed publication
  • Optimize for smaller organs with limited training data
