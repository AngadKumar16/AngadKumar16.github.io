Automated Cardiac Vascular Network Mapping from CT Angiography Using U-NET CNN for 3D Bioprinting

3D U-Net convolutional neural network for patient-specific vascular modeling

Angad Kumar · Tompkins High School
Mrinal Yalageri · Tompkins High School

Download Poster PDF

Overview

We developed a 3D U-Net convolutional neural network that automatically extracts cardiac vascular networks from CT angiography and converts them into bioprint-ready 3D models, enabling faster, consistent, and patient-specific vascular modeling for implantable heart tissue.

Background

Cardiovascular disease causes 17.9 million deaths per year worldwide. Key challenges include:

  • 13 Americans die daily awaiting organ transplants
  • 86% of bioprinted constructs fail in 7 days from vascular necrosis
  • Manual annotation of CT scans takes 8-12 hours per patient

Engineering Methodology

Phase A: 3D U-Net Model

  • DICOM alignment preserved
  • Original voxel spacing kept (no distortion)
  • Center of Mass validation
  • CT window: [-200, 700] HU

Phase B: STL Conversion

  • Base 3D mesh generation
  • Taubin smoothing
  • Wall thickness enforcement (≥3mm)
  • Final STL output
Model Workflow: Phase A (3D U-Net) and Phase B (STL Conversion)

Results

Geometry Integrity

Watertight models with zero holes, manifold topology with perfect edge connectivity, and validated sliceability across 50 planes.

Wall Thickness

≥3mm enforced for PLGA structural requirements, preventing slicing failures and ensuring continuous extrusion.

Print Ready

Direct workflow from diagnostic imaging to bioprinting, reducing production time from days to hours.

Impact & Future Work

Impact: Reduces 3D bioprinting time from days to hours, allowing radiologists to focus on surgeries rather than manual annotation. Addresses organ shortage crisis and improves tissue survival rates.
  • Expand to lymphatic networks and multi-organ applications
  • Integrate MRI for increased accessibility
  • Optimize for smaller organs with limited data
  • Clinical validation and peer-reviewed publication
